## INAV 9.1.0-RC1 Available

**9.1 is a maintenance release** focused on reliability, safety, and GPS accuracy. **Fully compatible with settings from 9.0.x** — your `diff all` loads cleanly.

### ✨ What's New

1. 🛡️ **Auto-Backup & Settings Migration**
   Configurator now backs up your config before every flash and auto-migrates renamed settings on upgrade

2. 🧭 **Magless Heading (Multirotors/VTOLs)**
   Point toward North before takeoff — nav modes (PosHold, RTH, WP) activate immediately without flying first to acquire a GPS heading

3. 📡 **GNSS Constellation Optimizer**
   Smarter update rate vs. constellation balancing on M8/M9/M10 modules for better GPS accuracy

4. 🔧 **Position Estimator Improvements**
   Better altitude hold in nav modes when GPS signal fluctuates

5. **Settings Save Fix**
   Configurator more reliabily saves certain settings, such as on the Advanced Tuning tab

### 🔒 Safety Fixes
- RTH Trackback FC lockup fixed
- 4-in-1 ESC random motor spin-up after disarm fixed (BLHeli_32 ≤ 32.9)
- Reversed throttle servo wrong disarm position fixed

### ⚠️ GPS Defaults Changed
`gps_ublox_nav_hz` now defaults to **8 Hz** (was 10); Galileo + BeiDou now **ON** by default. No action needed if these are already in your `diff all`.
