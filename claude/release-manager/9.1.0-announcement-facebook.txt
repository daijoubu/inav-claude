INAV 9.1.0 — Available Now

INAV 9.1 is a maintenance release focused on reliability, safety, and GPS accuracy. No breaking changes from 9.0.x — your saved configuration loads cleanly without any edits needed, and no chip erase is required.

Since 9.0.1: 78 fixes and 115 new features/enhancements across firmware and configurator, including 19 new hardware targets.

HIGHLIGHTS

Auto-Backup & Settings Migration
The configurator now automatically backs up your configuration before every firmware flash and can migrate renamed settings when upgrading. A safety net built right in.

Magless Heading for Multirotors and VTOLs
Point your multicopter or VTOL toward North before takeoff and navigation modes (Position Hold, RTH, Waypoints) will activate immediately — no need to fly first to acquire a GPS heading.

GPS Accuracy Improvements
Smarter GNSS constellation management for M8, M9, and M10 GPS modules. Better balance between update rate and the number of tracked satellites for improved position accuracy.

MSP Connection Stability
Configurator load stalls fixed, with more reliable connect and reconnect recovery.

Safety Fixes
• RTH Trackback flight controller lockup fixed
• Random motor spin-up after disarming fixed (4-in-1 ESCs with BLHeli_32 32.9 and earlier)
• Reversed throttle servo outputting wrong position when disarmed fixed
• SDIO blocking delays on problematic SD cards fixed

New Hardware
ICM-45686 IMU, MS5525DSO airspeed sensor, 19 new flight controller targets including AIKONF4V2, AXISFLYINGH743PRO, BLUEBERRYH743, Brahma H7, HAKRCH743, and TBS_LUCID_H7_V3.

GPS DEFAULTS CHANGED
The GPS update rate now defaults to 8 Hz (was 10 Hz), and Galileo and BeiDou constellations are now enabled by default. If you had these settings explicitly saved in your configuration, no change is needed.

Thanks to everyone who tested the Release Candidate and reported issues — you made this release better! Download links in the first comment.

Happy Flying!
